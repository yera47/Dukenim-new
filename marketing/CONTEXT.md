# Dukenim marketing context

Last reviewed: 2026-08-14

## Audience

Owners and sellers of small Kazakhstan retail businesses who currently sell through Instagram Direct, WhatsApp, an offline shop, and spreadsheets. Priority hypotheses include clothing, footwear, cosmetics, jewelry, children’s goods, gifts, flowers, home goods, furniture, pet goods, auto parts, and building materials. Category and city priority must be validated with real acquisition data.

## Positioning hypothesis

Primary working idea: **«Instagram — это лента. Dukenim — это каталог, который продаёт сам.»**

Explain value through a clear catalog, customer self-selection/order, and one owner workflow. Do not use “website” as the main differentiator. It may be used factually when technically or legally necessary.

This positioning is a testable marketing hypothesis, not an immutable fact. Measure registrations and qualified conversations, then refine it.

## Message pillars

1. The customer sees the assortment and orders without a long Direct conversation.
2. Products, orders, stock, and customers remain in one operating flow.
3. The owner can perform frequent work from a phone.
4. Dukenim is locally understandable for Kazakhstan and uses KZT.

## Evidence rules

- Do not invent customers, testimonials, revenue, conversion rates, market share, or performance results.
- 2GIS market-count claims: source CSVs and methodology are now present in `marketing/research/` (added 2026-08-14). City/category size ordering is directional; chain and "already has a website" exclusion is not yet complete (see `marketing/research/README.md`) — do not treat raw counts as a final addressable-market number until that pass is audited.
- Do not claim that real online payment is connected.
- Show real product behavior with demonstration data that contains no personal information.

## Channels and production

- Instagram is primary; TikTok receives an adapted version without another platform’s watermark.
- Prefer real screen recordings, owner/founder voice or face, subtitles, and reusable templates.
- Generative video is optional and requires an approved brief and cost before purchase.
- TikTok public API posting requires its own app approval/audit; begin with manual publication.

## Approval gate

Ideas → owner approves content plan → scripts/assets are produced → owner approves final media → publish/schedule → record metrics at 24 and 72 hours.

Never auto-publish unapproved material. Never store social passwords, access tokens, customer data, or API secrets in this folder.

Routine technical preparation is pre-authorized and does not require repeated confirmation. Always request confirmation immediately before publicly saving profile changes, publishing or deleting social content, scheduling newly approved content, or spending money. The owner personally completes login, OTP, and OAuth account authorization.
