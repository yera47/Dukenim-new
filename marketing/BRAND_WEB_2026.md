# Dukenim — web identity, September 2026

This digital specification supersedes the old gold/room direction for platform interfaces. It does not replace tenant-selected storefront palettes or claim the historical PDF was regenerated.

## Identity

Use the original doorway D from `public/brand/dukenim-flat-symbol.png`. The web component displays it in solid black or white. Preserve its proportions and transparent negative space. Align the 25 px symbol optically with the 23 px, 800-weight Manrope wordmark; use 8 px separation and one shared line height. Do not enlarge the symbol independently of the wordmark. At compact sizes show only D.

## Colour and hierarchy

White surfaces, #171717 primary ink, #626262 secondary ink, #e3e3e3 borders, #fafafa work area. Use a single filled black primary action per local decision group. Validation errors may retain semantic red. Product photographs and merchant palettes keep their own colours.

## Motion

Transitions explain selection, submission and arrival of an order. Use opacity and transform, 180–600 ms, with visible pause/manual controls for repeating demonstrations. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Stop automatic loops off-screen and while the tab is hidden. Do not lock page scrolling.

## Mobile

Keep login and the principal action visible. Use one-column forms, at least 44 px touch controls and 16 px input text. Tables may scroll inside their container. Navigation exposes all cabinet modules through the existing More sheet. Keep bottom actions clear of safe-area insets.
