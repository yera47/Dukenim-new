- 2026-08-17: Catalog creation includes the first design choice. Each public tariff offers two distinct curated storefront templates and curated palettes; «Бренд» also allows a brand accent colour.
- 2026-08-17: Claude Code must turn substantial owner requests into a repository-grounded, structured Codex brief before delegation, preserving the owner’s explicit constraints and acceptance criteria.
- 2026-08-18: The approved Stage 2 marketing hierarchy is: eyebrow «Dukenim — продажи 24/7», core promise «Одна ссылка вместо сотни сообщений», then proof that a customer chooses and orders in the catalog while the owner manages products, stock, and orders in one cabinet. Do not use «каталог в один клик» as the primary promise.
- 2026-08-18: First-run flow is confirmed: registration creates only the account; the owner chooses store niche and adds a phone number while creating the catalog. Show a neutral seven-day full-access state rather than unexplained tariff locks after registration. Brand shows curated palettes first, then an optional custom brand colour; Start shows Brand-only features as explained locked previews. Catalogs may be saved as drafts. Publication requires a name, at least one priced product, and a contact method; delivery may be configured later and must not appear publicly while absent.
- 2026-08-18: Catalog design system is confirmed: launch with four foundations — Start «Ателье» and «Маркет», Brand «Журнал» and «Галерея». Allow safe template and palette changes after publication without losing products, orders, or storefront address, using a preview-before-apply draft. Scale through niche-specific presets first, then five variations per niche rather than separate unmaintainable codebases.
- 2026-08-18: Buyer checkout stays guest-first. Both tariffs must expose a truthful Kaspi Pay setup centre: no passwords or payment credentials are collected by Dukenim; public online payment appears only after a real verified provider integration. Kaspi QR at pickup is an owner-configured option. Add a separate reservation flow: reserve stock temporarily, let the owner confirm it, expire it automatically by configurable duration, then convert it to an offline sale at pickup.
- 2026-08-18: CRM uses the confirmed order/reservation statuses and four employee roles. Offline sales must support a fast multi-line cart: one sale can contain many products, variants such as sizes/colours, and quantities; the customer remains optional.
- 2026-08-18: Employee roles are editable permission presets, not fixed restrictions. The owner can invite staff, choose a common position or create any custom title, then grant/revoke module-level access at any time. Provide simple module levels and an advanced per-module editor; preserve owner-only control over ownership, subscription/billing, payment connection, and irreversible account actions.
- 2026-08-18: Launch a real mobile application alongside the web cabinet, sharing the same backend and permissions; push notifications and camera barcode scanning are first-release requirements. Owners may invite a staff member before the catalog is complete and grant catalog/product/media onboarding access, so a content manager can set up the first catalog. Add barcode/SKU and label-printing support to the product roadmap; do not conflate a barcode label printer with fiscal receipt equipment.
- 2026-08-18: Defer barcode label printers, fiscal receipts, hardware gifts, and their commercial claims. They must not be implemented, shown in tariffs, or promised to customers until a separate owner decision after hardware and economic validation.
- 2026-08-20: Root administration is confirmed as a separate superadmin-only control centre: platform health, shop lifecycle, funnel analytics, support queue, tariff/feature management, template rollout, immutable sensitive-action audit, and backup/incident state. Staff impersonation, tariff changes, suspension/restoration, and data exports require a reason and audit entry. Start with in-app and email support; do not automate WhatsApp communications without a compliant official integration. Root access remains owner-only initially; any later operational administrator is restricted from ownership, billing, payment connections, and irreversible account actions.
- 2026-08-20: Dukenim will prepare for a direct public production launch rather than a separately branded beta. The release gate remains mandatory: all critical customer, owner, staff, root, backup, legal, and monitoring flows must be verified before opening registration. Subscription checkout must support server-validated promotional codes, created and tracked only from root administration; a code may grant a defined discount, free period, or plan entitlement but cannot bypass payment-provider verification or be stacked without an explicit future decision.
